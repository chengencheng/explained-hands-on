# StudyCrew — One-Page Product Plan
# StudyCrew 一页纸产品方案（示例）

Author 作者: Product Explained example  Date 日期: Sept 2026  Version 版本: 1
*A made-up example for teaching. 为教学编写的虚构示例。*

---

### 1 · Problem 问题
Before a big test, students want to study together, but organizing it is a mess. Someone asks "anyone want to study?" in the class group chat; ten people reply "maybe"; nobody meets.
大考之前，同学们想一起复习，可组织起来一团糟。有人在班级群里问"有人一起复习吗？"，十个人回复"也许吧"，结果谁也没见上面。

### 2 · Users & real need 用户与真正的需求
- **Users 用户:** high school students in the week before a test (start with one grade at one school). 考试前一周的高中生（先从一所学校的一个年级开始）。
- **Interviews 采访（5 people）:** "I only want people on the same chapter." "We never agree on a time." "Studying alone, I don't know if I'm ready." 「我只想找复习同一章的人。」「我们永远定不下时间。」「一个人复习，不知道自己准备好没有。」
- **Real job 真正的任务:** not "a group", but **feel ready for the test**. 不是"一个小组"，而是**考前心里有底**。

### 3 · Value 价值
- **Old way 旧体验:** the class group chat or studying alone: messy, but free, and everyone is already there. 班级群或一个人复习：乱，但免费，而且大家本来都在群里。
- **New way 新体验:** a real plan (same chapter, fixed time, fixed place) in about a minute. 大约一分钟得到一个真正的计划：同一章、固定时间、固定地点。
- **Switching cost 替换成本:** downloading a new app is too much, so v1 is **a link that opens right inside the group chat**; no account, no download. 下载新 App 成本太高，所以第一版是**一个在群聊里直接打开的链接**：不注册、不下载。

### 4 · One sentence 一句话定位
For high school students preparing for a test, StudyCrew matches you with classmates studying the same chapter this week. Unlike the class group chat, it turns "anyone want to study?" into a real plan in one minute.
对于准备考试的高中生，StudyCrew 帮你找到这周复习同一章的同学。和班级群不同，它能在一分钟内把一句"有人一起复习吗？"变成一个真正的计划。

### 5 · Smallest test 最小的测试
- **How 做法:** one test week. Share an online form (subject, chapter, free times). Match students **by hand** into groups of 3–4 and message each group a time and place. 一个考试周：分享在线表格（科目、章节、空闲时间），**手动**把同学分成 3 到 4 人的小组，再给每组发时间和地点。
- **Success 成功标准:** at least 10 sign-ups **and** at least 3 groups actually meet. 至少 10 人报名，**并且**至少 3 个小组真的见面。
- **Biggest risk tested 检验的最大风险:** value: will students actually show up? 价值风险：同学们真的会来吗？

### 6 · Core flow 核心流程
1. Open the link from the group chat. 从群聊里打开链接。
2. Pick subject + chapter, tick free times. 选科目和章节，勾选空闲时间。
3. Get "Your crew: Thu 4 pm, library 2F, with Mia and Leo." 收到"你的小组：周四下午 4 点，图书馆 2 楼，和 Mia、Leo 一起"。

### 7 · Numbers 指标
- **North Star 北极星指标:** study sessions that actually happen each week. 每周真正发生的复习次数。
- **Vanity metric to ignore 要忽略的虚荣指标:** total sign-ups. 注册总人数。
- **Growth loop 增长循环:** every new group invites 2–3 classmates through the same link. 每个新小组通过同一个链接，邀请两三个同学。
- **Retention 留存:** do students come back before the next test? 下一次考试前，同学们还会回来吗？

### 8 · First-week operations 第一周的运营
Remind the class 3 days before the test; after each session ask "Was it useful? Coming next time?"; write down repeated questions for version 2.
考前三天在群里提醒一次；每次复习后问一句"有用吗？下次还来吗？"；记下大家反复问的问题，准备第二版。

### 9 · Money 钱
Free, as a school club project, for now. No money is needed to run the test. If it grows, a school or tutoring-center license is possible, but only if it truly helps students study better.
先作为免费的学校社团项目。做测试不需要花钱。如果以后做大了，可以考虑向学校或辅导机构授权收费，前提是它真的帮同学们学得更好。

### 10 · Responsibility 责任
- **Who could be harmed 谁可能受到伤害:** students left out of groups; anyone judged by their grades. 被落下、没分到组的同学；被人拿成绩评判的同学。
- **Data 数据:** first name, class, subject, chapter, free times. Nothing else; deleted after the test week. 名字、班级、科目、章节、空闲时间；别的一概不收；考试周结束后删除。
- **We will NOT 我们不会:** show public ratings of students, build an endless feed, or let strangers join (classmates only). 公开给同学打分、做没有尽头的信息流、让陌生人加入（只限同学）。

---

### Next step 下一步
Run the one-week test. If it works, build version 1 (the link-based web app) in the vibe coding series.
先做一周的测试。如果成功，就在 vibe coding 系列里把第一版（基于链接的网页应用）做出来。
